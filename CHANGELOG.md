# BharatVera changelog

## V1.0.7
- New green-and-cream design from your concept images; bottom bar is now Home, Shop, centre Cart button, Diary and Profile.
- Simple home: search with voice, calories remaining, quick actions (Scan, Diet plan, Cook, Orders, Progress, Rewards, Assistant, Offers), steps and water, banner, categories, health partner tip, special offers.
- Smart search: autocomplete, voice search, filters (fits my diet, no red flags, high protein, under Rs 100, dishes or products, category), recent searches.
- Shop: category tiles and category pages.
- Food diary like MyFitnessPal: calories remaining = goal - food + exercise, macros, breakfast / lunch / snacks / dinner logging with search, my plan, recent and quick add, exercise log, steps counted as exercise, water, weight log, logging streak, any of the last 7 days.
- Nutrition assistant: suggests healthy products, shows nutrition facts, helps improve your diet, tracks your order.
- Offers and rewards: HEALTHY10, FIRST50, MEAL20 and 200 points for Rs 50 off; points from delivered orders, food logging, scans and achievements; daily challenge; 14 achievements.
- Product page: benefits and origin, "Log in my diary".
- Orders: live tracker line and one-tap reorder.
- Progress: health score card and weight trend.
- Text size setting (small to extra large).

## V1.0.6
- Step counter rebuilt: it now measures every phone's motion correctly at any speed, counts walking accurately (within about 3% in testing), ignores shakes and standing still, saves each step straight away, and restarts when you come back to the app. The Steps card shows "Sensor live", "Waiting for motion data" or a fix-it message, so you can see whether the phone is sending data.
- New look based on your videos: soft white background with a gentle green and orange glow, white rounded cards, forest-green rings, orange accents, small caps labels.
- Diet plan: week day selector, macro rings (planned vs target), meal times and protein/carb/fat chips; future days are previews.
- Product report: score ring, verdict tag, red-flag and good-point tags, five weighted checks.
- Progress: health score trend graph with a green 70+ band, 14-day consistency grid, goals met with check circles.
- The background setup screen now matches the installed web app: Allow notifications (with a test notification), turn off "Pause app activity if unused", set battery to Unrestricted, and autostart. It states clearly that a web app cannot count steps while closed.
- The phone-app version of the screen keeps the "Open settings" buttons.

## V1.0.5
- Camera: if the live camera is blocked (for example inside the Claude preview), the Scan page explains why and offers "Take a photo of the QR code", which works everywhere.
- Camera errors now say whether permission was denied or the preview blocks the camera.
- New "Count steps in the background" screen (Profile): allow physical activity, run in the background (unrestricted battery) and turn on autostart. The Home screen asks about it once.
- The version number is shown on the login page and on the Profile page.
- Native app kit: buttons that open the Android battery and autostart settings.

## V1.0.4
- Separate demo emails for admin, restaurant owner, grocery warehouse manager, packer and delivery partner.
- Warehouse packers prepare grocery orders; the manager adds packers.
- Customers order their preferred grocery quantity (grams or pieces) within the stock, step and minimum set by the admin or warehouse.
